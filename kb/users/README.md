# End users

Status: **Seeded**. Last updated: 2026-11-04. Builds on the
[idea section](../idea/README.md).

## What we already know

**Primary users (Decision D-13, proposed):** close friend groups of 3–8,
roughly 18–30 years old. University friends, hostel mates and young
professionals who already share a group chat, living together or far apart.
They probably already play party games, share daily-game results or post
year-end recaps.

**First market (Q-07, open):** university friend groups, starting with the
team's own college.

### Roles inside a circle

| Role | Who | What they need | Design implication |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Organiser** | Starts group chats and plans things | Quick setup, one invite for everyone | Onboarding is built to win them |
| **Regular** | Plays most days | A fun two-minute round, a reason to come back | Daily drop, league, twist cards |
| **Quiet member** | Shares less, may feel exposed | Control and invisibility | Invisible pass, rest days, no free-text |
| **Long-distance friend** | In another city or time zone | To feel included without being online at the same time | 24-hour window, catch-up card |

If the design works for the quiet member, it works for everyone.

### Not for

- **Under-18s** at launch (Q-03).
- People with a difficult relationship to tracking or body data.
- Large public communities. The closeness depends on small circles.
