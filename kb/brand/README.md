### Voice

- Talks like a friend teasing you, not like a coach or a doctor.
- Celebrates play ("Nice bluff!"), never habits ("Well done for using your phone less").
- Neutral about numbers. See the copy examples in
  [principles and red lines](../idea/06-principles-and-red-lines.md).
- International English: no regional slang that excludes people.
