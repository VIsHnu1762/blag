## The idea in one paragraph

BLAG is a private social game for close friend groups of 3 to 8. Each day, one
friend makes a claim about their own habits and may be bluffing. The others
vote True or Bluff, and the real number is revealed. Points reward reading
your friends well, not having "good" habits. Daily points build into a monthly
league that ends in a finale where everyone is recognised. It takes about ten
minutes a day, has no public feed, and never ranks anyone's health.

## The three things most likely to change this idea

1. **Apple doesn't let apps export Screen Time numbers** (see
   [mobile dependencies](../mobile-dependencies/README.md)). On iPhone, stats
   will probably always be typed in by the player. The game has to be fun and
   fair with typed-in numbers.
2. **People don't know their own numbers.** Research shows most people
   misjudge their screen time, usually underestimating it. That is a problem
   for accuracy, but it's also a game in its own right (see the "Guess
   yourself" idea in the [idea bank](05-idea-bank.md)).
3. **Daily social apps often spike and fade.** BeReal, Gas and tbh all grew
   very fast and then lost most of their users. BLAG needs a reason to stay,
   not only a reason to try it.
