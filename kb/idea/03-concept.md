# 03 · Concept

Status: **Proposal**. Last updated: 2026-11-04. This describes the experience,
not the final rules. Exact scoring and timers belong in the game design
documents once this is agreed.

## The shape of BLAG

```
 Circle (3–8 friends, invite-only, lasts across seasons)
   └── Season = one calendar month
         ├── Daily drop (every day, ~10 minutes, 24-hour window)
         │     └── 1–2 rounds: claim → vote → reveal → points
         ├── Weekly mirror (private to each player)
         └── Monthly finale (shared): moments, titles, recap card
```

## 1. The circle

- A **persistent** group of 3–8 friends. It is not a one-off room.
- **Invite-only**, through a link, a QR code or picking friends one at a time.
  No address-book upload in the MVP (Decision D-07).
- Friends may be in the same hostel or in different countries. One daily drop
  per circle, with a window long enough to reach every time zone (D-11).

## 2. The daily drop: one round

| Step | What happens | Feeling we want |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Drop | The day's prompt arrives, e.g. "Today's Speaker: Priya · Topic: steps yesterday" | Curiosity |
| 2. Seal | The Speaker's real number is **sealed** first (typed in, or read from the phone where possible) | Fairness |
| 3. Claim | The Speaker states a number. It may be true or a bluff | Mischief |
| 4. Guess | Everyone else votes **True** or **Bluff**, and may also guess the real number | Suspense |
| 5. Reveal | The sealed number is revealed with an animation and a **TRUTH** or **BLUFF!** stamp | The peak moment |
| 6. React | Preset emoji reactions only. No free-text during rounds | Laughter, never pile-ons |
| 7. Points | Points go to good reads, good bluffs and close guesses | Skill, not habits |

**Seal before you claim** (proposal P-14) is the answer to fake stats when
numbers are typed in: you can bluff in your *claim*, but you can't quietly
change your *truth* after seeing the votes.

Who speaks rotates through the circle so everyone gets a turn across a season.

## 3. The monthly league

- Daily points add up across the month. The month is a **season**.
- Every season starts fresh, so nobody carries a bad month forward.
- Points come from **skill** (reading people, bluffing well, guessing close),
  never from the stat values themselves. Nobody is structurally last because
  of their habits (D-05).
- The **finale** at the end of the month celebrates moments and play styles,
  not only rank. Every member gets a title (D-06).

Example finale titles: *Poker Face* (best bluffer), *Mind Reader* (best
guesser), *Open Book* (always honest), *Wildcard* (most surprising reveal).
Titles describe how someone **played**, never what their habits were. Titles
like "Night Owl" or "The Lurker" are out (see [principles](06-principles-and-red-lines.md)).

## 4. Personal and league: connected, but neutral

| | My month (private) | Our month (shared) |
|---|---|---|
| Who sees it | Only me | The circle |
| Compared against | **My own baseline** | Nothing. It's a story, not a ranking |
| Shows | Gentle notes like "different from usual" | Best bluff, closest guess, funniest reveal |
| Never shows | Red/green, up/down arrows, "good/bad" | Anyone's raw history |

The link between the two is **focus lanes**:

- Each player picks a focus: fitness, less social media, sleep, productivity,
  or "just for fun".
- Their claims come from their focus, so the circle learns what each friend is
  working on without anyone ranking it.
- Whether focus goals ever affect league points is still open (Q-02). The
  current lean is **no**.

## 5. Absence and passing

| Situation | What happens |
|---|---|
| Missed the drop | You're just late. The round stays open for 24 hours |
| Away for days | Optional **sealed claims** left in advance |
| Came back | A short catch-up card of what you missed |
| What friends see | A neutral "away" status. Never a missed-day count |
| Scoring | No penalty, no pity points |
| Don't want to share today | **Pass**, invisibly. A pass looks the same as a random skip |
| Several passes in a row | Privately offered "non-stat day" prompts, never announced to the group |

## 6. Days without stats

To make sure the ritual never depends on numbers, some days use other prompts:

- **Rest day** (weekly): a non-stat prompt, e.g. "Who in the circle would
  survive longest without their phone?"
- Later, other **daily card types** from the idea bank: movie ratings, memes,
  music. These come only after the core round works (D-09).

## 7. The ten-minute budget

| Part | Target time |
|---|---|
| Opening the drop and seeing the prompt | under 30 seconds |
| Speaker seals and claims | under 2 minutes |
| Each voter votes and guesses | under 1 minute |
| Reveal and reactions | 1–2 minutes |
| Reading the day's recap | 1–2 minutes |

Remote circles play **asynchronously** inside the 24-hour window. Circles in
the same room can play **live**, with everyone's phone as a controller (an idea
borrowed from Jackbox, see the [inspiration library](04-inspiration-library.md)).
