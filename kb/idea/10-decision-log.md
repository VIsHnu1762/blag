# 10 · Decision log

Last updated: 2026-11-04. A decision is recorded here once the team has agreed
it. Each entry says what was decided, the alternatives, and why.

| ID | Date | Decision | Alternatives considered | Reason | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| D-01 | 2026-11-04 | Product title is **BLAG: Are You Bluffing?** | "Trust Me, Bro", "Caught You!", "Ten Minutes with Your People" | Short, memorable, and a line friends actually say | Agreed |
| D-02 | 2026-11-01 | "BLAG" was chosen for being unique and recognisable, **not** as British slang; copy stays neutral and international | British-flavoured copy ("mates", "blagging") | Wider audience; matches the team's intent | Agreed |
| D-03 | 2026-11-01 | BLAG is a **daily ritual with a monthly league and finale**, not a one-off party game | 8–12-round sessions in temporary rooms | Builds habit and a reason to return | Agreed |
| D-04 | 2026-11-04 | Category: **private social game built around a daily friend ritual** | Social media; productivity app; gamified wellness tracker | Matches the experience and avoids health-ranking pressure | Agreed |
| D-05 | 2026-11-01 | League points come from **skill** (reading, bluffing, guessing), never from stat values | Points for "better" stats | Nobody is structurally last because of their habits | Proposed |
| D-06 | 2026-11-01 | **Every member gets a title** at the finale; titles describe play style, not habits | Rank-only finale | Nobody leaves with guilt or pride about habits | Proposed |
| D-07 | 2026-11-01 | **Contact sync** is optional, off by default and a later feature; MVP uses invite links, QR codes and a one-at-a-time contact picker | Address-book upload at signup | Contacts are third-party data; avoids exposing users | Proposed |
| D-08 | 2026-11-01 | **Missed days carry no penalty** and are never shown to the circle; a 24-hour window and catch-up card keep everyone involved | Streaks; missed-day counts | Agreed principle: everyone stays involved, no guilt | Agreed |
| D-09 | 2026-11-04 | Build **one great loop first**; other card types (movies, memes, music, productivity) come later | Build several card types at once | Avoids scope creep before the core is proven | Proposed |
| D-10 | 2026-11-01 | Personal and league layers are **connected through stories, not numbers**; personal data is measured only against your own baseline | Shared personal charts | Agreed principle: interconnected, nothing to feel guilt or pride about | Agreed |
| D-11 | 2026-11-01 | **One daily drop per circle** with a 24-hour window across time zones | A fixed clock time for everyone | Circles may span countries | Proposed |
| D-12 | 2026-11-01 | Align with **international standards** (GDPR, DPDP, WCAG 2.2 AA, OWASP MASVS, ISO documentation standards); claim alignment, not certification | No formal standards | Agreed requirement; see [the decisions workbook](design-decisions-2026-11-01.xlsx) | Agreed |
| D-13 | 2026-11-04 | Primary users are **close friend groups of 3–8, roughly 18–30**; first market likely university friend groups | Broad audience; teenagers | Dense, daily contact, easy to test with | Proposed (see Q-07) |

"Agreed" means the team stated it directly. "Proposed" means it came out of
the design discussions and still needs explicit team sign-off.
